01 — Company

A house of brands, not an agency.

RVSE LLC is a Delaware company operating out of Miami, Florida. We don't sell services and we don't take clients. We create, own and scale consumer brands in categories where the product has to do the work.

Every brand starts the same way: a category we understand, a formulation we can defend, and a customer we can reach profitably. We keep the whole stack in house — product, brand, media and retention — because that is where the compounding happens.

02 — Categories

Three categories. One thesis.

People buy supplements, wellness and sleep products for the same reason: they want tomorrow to feel different from today. Everything we build answers to that.

01

Supplements

Formulation-first products for people who actually read the label. Clinically-relevant doses, sourcing we can trace, and claims we are willing to defend in writing.

02

Wellness

Daily-use products that have to earn their place in a routine. Designed for the second order and the sixth, not for a one-time impulse buy.

03

Sleep

The category where the result is felt rather than measured. Products and rituals built around the eight hours that decide how the other sixteen go.

03 — Model

How an RVSE brand gets built.

The same five steps, in the same order, every time. Discipline in the sequence is most of the edge.

  1. 01

    Category research

    We start from demand that already exists and is badly served — search behaviour, review sections, and what people complain about after they have already bought something else.

  2. 02

    Product & formulation

    We spec the formula and work with vetted contract manufacturers. The product has to survive a side-by-side comparison with the category leader, on the label and in the hand.

  3. 03

    Brand & identity

    Name, positioning, packaging and storefront. The brand has to explain itself in three seconds, on a phone, to someone who has never heard of it.

  4. 04

    Acquisition

    Creative-led paid media. We test angles rather than audiences, and we produce the creative in house so the loop between insight and asset stays short.

  5. 05

    Retention

    Subscription, replenishment and lifecycle. The first order buys the customer; the second one is where a DTC brand turns into a business.
